About CV Studio Pro

A simple, honest CV builder made for job seekers in Pakistan. Start free, and pay only if you want a premium design.

What is CV Studio Pro?

CV Studio Pro is a website where you type your details once and see your CV update live. You can choose from 64 designs, change the design at any time without losing your details, and download your CV as a PDF or a Word file. You do not need to create an account.

Who is it for?

Freshers and students

Simple layouts and ready-made summary suggestions help you make your first CV without design skills.

Working professionals

Clean, professional designs for office, sales, technical, medical and other jobs, with sections in the order employers expect.

People who want a stronger look

Premium designs with photo frames, colour themes and modern layouts, ordered with simple manual support.

What is free and what is premium?

Free

The builder, live preview, free templates, PDF download and editable Word download. No sign-up needed.

Premium

You can try any premium design with your own details for free. To download it, you order and receive an access code.

How a premium order works

You pay, send your order, we confirm the payment manually, and we send you your access code. See the Pricing and Refund Policy pages for the details.

How to make a CV

  1. Pick a designBrowse the templates by style and tap one you like.
  2. Fill your detailsGo through the builder step by step. The CV on the right updates as you type.
  3. DownloadPress Download PDF and choose Save as PDF. Need help? Read the step-by-step guide.

Your privacy

Your CV is built in your own browser. Your details are sent to us only when you choose to send an order. We do not sell your data. Read the full Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.

Tell us what to improve

This website is improved based on feedback from real users. If something is confusing, a template looks wrong, or you want a new feature, please tell us on the Contact page. We use your feedback to decide what to improve next.
