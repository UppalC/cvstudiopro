How to prepare for a job interview

A little preparation makes a big difference. Use this checklist before your next interview.

Updated November 2026 · Written by the CV Studio Pro team · All CV tips

Mukhtasar: Company ke bare mein parh kar jayen, aam sawalon ke jawab soch kar rakhein, documents saath rakhein aur waqt par pahunchein.

Before the interview

  1. Read the job advertisement again. Note the skills and duties that are asked for, and think of one real example from your life for each.
  2. Learn about the company. Find out what it does, what it sells and where it works. Check its website and its official pages.
  3. Know your CV well. Be ready to explain every line, including dates and reasons for leaving a job.
  4. Plan the journey. Find the address, check the route and leave early. For an online interview, test your internet, camera and microphone, and choose a quiet place.
  5. Prepare your documents. Bring a few printed copies of your CV, your original certificates (and photocopies) and your ID if the employer asked for them.

Questions you can expect

  • “Tell me about yourself.” Give a short answer of about a minute: who you are, your recent experience or studies, and why you are applying.
  • “Why do you want this job?” Connect your skills with what the job needs. Avoid answers about salary only.
  • “What are your strengths?” Pick two that match the job and give an example for each.
  • “What is your weakness?” Choose a real, small weakness and say what you are doing to improve it.
  • “Why did you leave your last job?” Stay honest and positive. Do not criticise your old employer.
  • “Do you have any questions for us?” Have two ready, such as about the daily work, the team, or how success is measured in this role.

A simple way to answer experience questions

Use the short Situation, Action, Result pattern. Describe the situation in one sentence, what you did, and what happened. For example: “Our records were often late (situation). I made a simple daily checklist and updated it every evening (action). Reports were then ready on time every week (result).” Only tell true stories.

On the day

  • Arrive 10 to 15 minutes early.
  • Dress neatly in a way that suits the workplace.
  • Switch your phone to silent.
  • Greet everyone politely, listen to the full question and answer clearly.
  • If you do not know something, say so honestly and explain how you would find out.

Salary questions

Know the usual pay range for the role in your city before you go, using reliable sources. If asked what you expect, give a reasonable figure or range, and show you are open to discussion.

After the interview

  • Thank the interviewer politely, in person and, if suitable, by a short email the same day.
  • Write down the questions you were asked. It helps you improve for the next interview.
  • Be careful of any job offer that asks you to pay money. A genuine employer does not charge you to hire you.
