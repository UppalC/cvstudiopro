Free ATS CV checker

Paste your CV text and see what to fix before you apply.

Apni CV ka text paste karein aur dekhein kya kami hai. Sab kuch aap ke browser mein hota hai.

Your text stays in your browser. It is not uploaded or saved.

What this checker looks at

  • Name, email and phone number are easy to find.
  • Clear headings for Experience, Education and Skills.
  • Length, bullet points, and whether achievements have numbers.
  • Signs that the text came from a table or columns, which some ATS software reads in the wrong order.
  • If you paste a job ad: how many of its main words appear in your CV.

What it cannot do

This is a simple rule-based checklist that runs in your browser. It is not a real ATS and it does not use AI, so a full pass does not guarantee that a company's system will accept your CV, and it cannot judge how well your experience matches a job. Use it to catch common mistakes, then read your CV once more yourself.

How to get the text from your CV

  1. Open your CV PDF or Word file.
  2. Select all the text (Ctrl+A on a computer) and copy it (Ctrl+C).
  3. Paste it in the first box above (Ctrl+V).

If the pasted text looks jumbled or empty, your file may be a picture or use columns, which is itself a warning sign for ATS software. Read more in What is an ATS CV and How to write a CV.
