Canadian resume format

In Canada people usually say “resume” for the document you send to employers. The Government of Canada’s Job Bank gives clear advice. Here it is in plain words.

Updated November 2026 · Written by the CV Studio Pro team · All CV tips

Mukhtasar: Canada mein resume 2 page tak, bina photo, bina umar/shadi/mazhab ke. SIN number kabhi na likhein. References sirf maangne par alag se dein.

The main rules from Job Bank

Job Bank, a Government of Canada service, advises the following:

  • Limit the resume to two pages, and focus on your most recent experience. Play down jobs older than about 15 years.
  • Do not include a photo. It is not the norm in Canada and can lower your chances.
  • Leave out age, weight, height, marital status, religion and political views.
  • Never include your Social Insurance Number. Never give an identity number on a resume.
  • Do not give your reasons for leaving past jobs.
  • Do not put references on the resume. Give them on a separate sheet only when asked.
  • Usually leave out hobbies unless they relate to the job.

Contact details

Put your name, address (city and province, or your location), phone number and a professional email at the top of page one. Use an email based on your name, without nicknames or numbers.

What to write

  • Show achievements, not just duties, and add real numbers: people supervised, percentage increases, sales or customers.
  • Use simple language and action verbs such as managed, led, developed, increased.
  • Keep each section to about five to seven bullet points.
  • Include volunteer or unpaid work when it is relevant.
  • Tailor the resume to each job description.
  • Proofread, and ask someone else to read it.

Coming from outside Canada

If you are applying from Pakistan or another country, remember that Canadian employers do not know your employers or institutions. Add a few words about them, write your degrees in full with the country, and describe your skills in plain English. Some newcomers add a short line about their work authorisation, but only write what is true. Immigration rules are separate from the resume, so check official government sources.

The big difference from Pakistan and the Gulf

A resume that works in Pakistan or the Gulf, with a photo, father’s name, date of birth and marital status, will look wrong in Canada. Remove those details. Compare with CV format in Pakistan and UK CV format.
