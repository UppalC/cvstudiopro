Cookie Policy

What we store in your browser, and the choice you control.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. What cookies and browser storage are

Cookies and similar technologies (like your browser's local storage) are small pieces of data saved on your device when you use a website.

2. What we use

Essential (always on)

The builder saves your work in your browser's local storage so you do not lose it when you refresh the page. We also store your cookie choice, your access code status, and a short-lived copy of site settings such as prices so pages load faster. These are needed for the website to work and stay on your device.

Analytics (only if you accept)

If you accept, we load Google Analytics (measurement ID G-P6MNCV79XR), which sets cookies to count visits and show us which pages are useful. If you decline, Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

3. Changing your choice

Use the Cookie settings button in the footer of any page to accept or decline analytics at any time. You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. Declining analytics does not stop the builder from working.

4. More information

See our Privacy Policy for how we handle personal information. For Google Analytics, see Google's own privacy information.

5. Contact

You can reach us by WhatsApp or email. Our current contact details are always shown on the Contact page.
