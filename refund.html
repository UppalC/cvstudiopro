Refund Policy

What happens if something goes wrong with your order.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Free use

The free builder and free templates cost nothing, so no refund applies to them.

2. Digital service

Premium orders are digital: you receive an access code that unlocks printing or saving a premium or customised design. Because the code gives immediate access once used, we cannot refund an order after the code has been used to download a finished CV, except where section 3 applies.

3. When you can get a refund

  • You paid but we could not confirm your payment or deliver an access code to you.
  • You paid twice for the same order.
  • The access code does not work and we are unable to fix the problem.
  • A fault on our side prevents you from downloading the design you paid for and we cannot resolve it.

4. When a refund is not available

  • You changed your mind after downloading your CV.
  • The CV content (spelling, dates, details) came from the information you entered.
  • You shared your access code with others or it was misused.
  • The payment was sent to the wrong account because the wrong details were typed. Please check the payment details on the order page before sending.

5. Changes instead of a refund

If you are unhappy with a layout or the wording, tell us. We will try to help you fix it, for example by switching template or adjusting details, before considering a refund.

6. How to request a refund

Contact us within 7 days of your payment with your order number (it starts with "CV-"), your name, and your Transaction ID. We will review the request and reply to you. Approved refunds are sent back to the account you paid from, usually within 7 working days, and the related access code is cancelled.

7. Contact

You can reach us by WhatsApp or email. Our current contact details are always shown on the Contact page.
