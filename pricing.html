Frequently asked questions

Is the CV builder really free?

Yes. You can use the builder, the live preview and the free templates without paying. Free CVs carry a small "Made with CV Studio Pro" line at the bottom.

What is a premium design and why does it need an access code?

Premium templates, custom colours and custom photo frames are premium designs. You can try them freely in the builder, but printing or saving a premium design as PDF needs an access code that you receive after your order is confirmed.

How do I order and pay?

Choose your design, press the Order button in the builder, fill in your details and submit the order. You will see our payment methods and your order number. Pay, enter the Transaction ID from your payment, and we will verify it.

How do I receive my access code?

After we verify your payment we send the access code by email, and we can also share it on WhatsApp. Orders are checked manually, so please keep your order number and Transaction ID ready if you contact us.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no account or password for customers. Your CV is saved in your own browser while you work. Use "Clear All Data" in the builder to remove it, especially on a shared computer.

Is my personal information safe?

Your CV stays in your browser until you choose to submit an order. We never ask for card numbers, PINs or passwords. Please read our Privacy Policy for details.

Should I add my CNIC, date of birth and father's name?

Only if the employer asks for them. These fields are optional and hidden by default, and you can switch the whole Personal Information section on or off at any time.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The builder and the preview work on phones. For the cleanest PDF you can also use a computer, and check the preview before printing.

What if I paid but have a problem?

Contact us with your order number and Transaction ID. Refund rules are explained on our Refund Policy page.
