Privacy Policy

We keep this simple: we use your information only to build your CV and handle your order.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Who we are

CV Studio Pro ("we", "us") is an online CV builder and premium CV design service. This policy explains what information we handle when you use this website.

2. Information we handle

  • CV content you type in the builder (name, contact details, experience, education, skills, optional personal details such as CNIC or date of birth, and an optional photo). While you only use the builder, this stays in your own browser storage on your device. We do not receive it.
  • Order details you choose to send us when you press "Submit Order Online" or contact us on WhatsApp: your name, WhatsApp number, email address, chosen template, notes, your CV text, and the payment Transaction ID you enter. This reaches us only when you submit it.
  • Payment proof such as a Transaction ID or screenshot you send us so we can match your payment. We never ask for card numbers, PINs, passwords or one-time codes.
  • Technical and usage data (pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location) collected by Google Analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies. See our Cookie Policy.

3. How we use it

  • To prepare, deliver and support your CV order, including sending you an access code and order updates by email or WhatsApp.
  • To verify payments and prevent misuse of access codes.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it (only with your consent for analytics).
  • To meet legal or accounting obligations where they apply.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use your CV content for advertising.

4. Who processes your data

We use trusted service providers to run the site: GitHub Pages (website hosting), Google Workspace services such as Google Sheets and Gmail (order records and emails), WhatsApp (messages you choose to send), and Google Analytics (if you consent). These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies and may store it outside Pakistan.

5. How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as needed to provide support and for reasonable record-keeping, and are then deleted or anonymised. Data stored in your browser stays there until you clear it, for example with the "Clear All Data" button in the builder.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the order information we hold about you, and you can withdraw analytics consent at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. To make a request, contact us using the details below and include your order number.

7. Security

We limit access to order records and use access codes and password protection for our admin area. No online service can promise perfect security, so please do not send sensitive documents you do not need to share. Share your CNIC only if you want it printed on your CV.

8. Children

This service is meant for job seekers aged 18 and over, or who are of legal working age in their country. We do not knowingly collect information from young children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

10. Contact

You can reach us by WhatsApp or email. Our current contact details are always shown on the Contact page.
