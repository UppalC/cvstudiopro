Privacy Policy

We keep this simple: we use your information only to build your CV and handle your order.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Who we are

CV Studio Pro ("we", "us") is an online CV builder and premium CV design service operated from Pakistan. This policy explains what information we handle when you use this website, why, and what rights you have.

2. Information we handle

  • CV content you type in the builder (name, contact details, experience, education, skills, optional personal details such as CNIC or date of birth, and an optional photo). While you only use the builder, this stays in your own browser storage on your device. We do not receive it.
  • Order details you choose to send us when you press "Submit Order Online" or contact us on WhatsApp: your name, WhatsApp number, email address, chosen template, notes, your CV text, and the payment Transaction ID you enter. This reaches us only when you submit it.
  • Payment proof such as a Transaction ID or screenshot you send us so we can match your payment. We never ask for card numbers, PINs, passwords or one-time codes.
  • Technical and usage data (pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location) collected by Google Analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies. See our Cookie Policy.

3. How we use it

  • To prepare, deliver and support your CV order, including sending you an access code and order updates by email or WhatsApp.
  • To verify payments and prevent misuse of access codes.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it (only with your consent for analytics).
  • To meet legal or accounting obligations where they apply.

We handle your order information because you gave it to us for your order (your consent) and because we need it to carry it out. Analytics run only with your consent.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use your CV content for advertising.

4. Who processes your data and where

We use service providers to run the site: GitHub Pages (website hosting), Google Workspace services such as Google Sheets and Gmail (order records and emails), WhatsApp (messages you choose to send), and Google Analytics (only if you consent). These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. Their servers may be outside Pakistan, for example in the United States, so your order information can be stored or processed in other countries. By submitting an order you agree to this. We do not sell your data.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order records for up to 12 months after the order is completed, so we can give support and handle disputes. After that we delete or anonymise them, unless we need to keep a record longer to resolve a dispute or to meet a legal duty. Data stored in your browser stays there until you clear it, for example with the "Clear All Data" button in the builder.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the order information we hold about you, to give you a copy of it, and to stop using it. You can withdraw analytics consent at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. If you ordered, you can also nominate someone to act for you. To make a request, contact us (see section 12) and include your order number. We aim to reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the data protection authority in your country where one exists.

7. Security and data breaches

We limit access to order records and use access codes and password protection for our admin area. No online service can promise perfect security, so please do not send sensitive documents you do not need to share. Share your CNIC or other ID number only if you want it printed on your CV. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell the people affected without undue delay and will inform the authority where the law requires it.

8. Children

This service is meant for job seekers aged 18 and over, or who are of legal working age in their country. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Where this service is offered

CV Studio Pro is operated from Pakistan and is intended for job seekers in Pakistan, India and the Gulf countries (Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman and Kuwait). It is not designed for people in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, and we do not accept paid orders from there. You may still read the website and use the free builder, which keeps your CV on your own device.

10. Laws in the countries we serve

  • Pakistan: We respect the right to privacy in the Constitution and the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016, and we follow the good practices of the proposed Personal Data Protection Bill (notice, purpose limits, security, access and correction).
  • India: If you are in India, we handle your personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules. We tell you what data we use and why, we ask for your consent for it, and you may access, correct and erase your data, withdraw consent, and make a complaint to us. We aim to resolve complaints within 90 days at most.
  • Saudi Arabia: If you are in Saudi Arabia, the Personal Data Protection Law applies to your data. You may ask to be informed about how we use it, to see it, to receive a copy, to correct it, to have it destroyed, and to withdraw your consent.
  • United Arab Emirates: If you are in the UAE, the Federal Personal Data Protection Law (Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) applies. We process your data on the basis of your consent or to carry out your order, and you may ask to access, correct or delete it.
  • Qatar, Bahrain, Oman and Kuwait: We apply the same notice, consent, access, correction and deletion practices described in this policy to users in these countries.

Where your local law gives you stronger rights than this policy, those rights are not reduced by this policy.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

12. Contact and complaints

You can reach us by WhatsApp or email for any privacy request or complaint. Our current contact details are always shown on the Contact page.
