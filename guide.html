How to use CV Studio Pro

Make a professional CV in 4 easy steps. No account needed. 4 asaan steps mein apni CV banayein.

Open the BuilderSee Templates
1

Start

Open the Builder. Press See an example first to see how a finished CV looks, or Start with my own details to begin with your own information. Use the filters (Free, Modern, ATS and more) to find a design, then tap its picture. You can also paste an old CV under Import old resume text.

Builder kholein. "See an example first" se namuna dekhein, ya "Start with my own details" se apni maloomat likhna shuru karein. Filters se design dhundein aur tasveer par tap karein. Purani CV ka text "Import old resume text" mein paste bhi kar sakte hain. Design baad mein bhi badal sakte hain, data safe rehta hai.

2

Details

Fill your name, job title, phone, email, address and a short summary. Add your photo (optional): use Zoom and Move to fit your face, and the Flip photo switch to mirror it.

Naam, job title, phone, email aur chhota summary likhein. Photo lagana zaroori nahi. Zoom aur Move se face theek karein.

3

Sections

Add Experience, Education and Skills. Use the Skill bars and Show % numbers switches if you like them. Show or hide sections you do not need. Put the newest job first.

Experience, Education aur Skills likhein. Jo section nahi chahiye use hide kar dein. Sab se naya kaam sab se upar likhein.

4

Design & Export

Check the live preview. Press Download PDF. In the print window choose Paper size A4, Margins None, Scale 100% and turn Background graphics on. You can also download Word (editable), a simple text file you can change, or Word (exact design), which keeps the design but is not editable text. To try another design later, open Change Template Anytime; your details stay.

Preview dekhein, phir Download PDF dabayen. A4, Margins None, Scale 100% aur Background graphics ON rakhein.

Free and premium designs

Free designs can be downloaded without payment and carry a small “Made with CV Studio Pro” line at the bottom. Premium designs can be tried freely in the builder; printing or saving a premium design as PDF needs an access code.

Free design bina payment download hota hai. Premium design ko PDF karne ke liye access code chahiye.

How to order a premium design

1

Choose and fill

Choose a premium design and fill your CV in the builder.

2

Press Start order

In the last step (Design & Export) press Start order (3 easy steps). Step 1 is Pay: see the payment options (EasyPaisa, JazzCash, bank and more), make the payment, then press I have paid / Next.

Aakhri step mein "Start order" dabayen. Pehle payment options dekh kar payment karein, phir "I have paid / Next" dabayen.

3

Send your order

Enter the Transaction ID if you have it, then press Submit Order Online (or send the order on WhatsApp). You get an Order Number. Send your payment screenshot with that number on WhatsApp. Your CV details go with the order, so you do not type them again.

Transaction ID likhein aur "Submit Order Online" dabayen. Order number milega; payment ka screenshot us number ke sath WhatsApp par bhejein.

4

Receive your access code

We check your payment manually and send the code by email and can also share it on WhatsApp. Open the order window, press I have a code, enter it, and download your premium PDF.

Payment verify hone ke baad code email ya WhatsApp par milta hai. Order window mein "I have a code" dabayen, code daal kar PDF download karein.

Use it like an app on your phone

You can add CV Studio Pro to your phone's home screen and open it like an app, with no app store and no download. On Android (Chrome), open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. On iPhone (Safari), tap the Share button and choose Add to Home Screen. After your first visit the builder also opens without internet. Premium orders and access codes still need internet.

Apne phone ki home screen par CV Studio Pro add karein aur app ki tarah kholein. Android Chrome: menu mein "Install app" ya "Add to Home screen". iPhone Safari: Share ka button, phir "Add to Home Screen". Pehli dafa site khol lene ke baad builder bina internet ke bhi khulta hai. Premium order aur access code ke liye internet chahiye.

Important: Your CV is saved in your own browser. Please do not clear browser data before downloading. If you change phone or clear data, contact us with your order number.

Tips for a better CV

Keep it to 1–2 pages. Use a clear photo with a plain background. Write results, not just duties. Add CNIC, date of birth or father’s name only if the employer asks. Check spelling before you print.
