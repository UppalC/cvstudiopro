How to use CV Studio Pro

Make a professional CV in 4 easy steps. No account needed. 4 asaan steps mein apni CV banayein.

Open the BuilderSee Templates
1

Start

Open the Builder. Press Fill Full Demo CV to see how a finished CV looks, or Start From Scratch to begin empty. Tap a design picture to choose your template.

Builder kholein. Demo dabayen ya khali se shuru karein. Pasand ka design tasveer par tap karke chunein. Design baad mein bhi badal sakte hain, data safe rehta hai.

2

Details

Fill your name, job title, phone, email, address and a short summary. Add your photo (optional): use Zoom and Move to fit your face, and the Flip photo switch to mirror it.

Naam, job title, phone, email aur chhota summary likhein. Photo lagana zaroori nahi. Zoom aur Move se face theek karein.

3

Sections

Add Experience, Education and Skills. Use the Skill bars and Show % numbers switches if you like them. Show or hide sections you do not need. Put the newest job first.

Experience, Education aur Skills likhein. Jo section nahi chahiye use hide kar dein. Sab se naya kaam sab se upar likhein.

4

Design & Export

Check the live preview. Press Print / Save PDF. In the print window choose Paper size A4, Margins None, Scale 100% and turn Background graphics on. You can also download an editable Word file.

Preview dekhein, phir Print / Save PDF dabayen. A4, Margins None, Scale 100% aur Background graphics ON rakhein.

Free and premium designs

Free designs can be downloaded without payment and carry a small “Made with CV Studio Pro” line at the bottom. Premium designs can be tried freely in the builder; printing or saving a premium design as PDF needs an access code.

Free design bina payment download hota hai. Premium design ko PDF karne ke liye access code chahiye.

How to order a premium design

1

Choose and fill

Choose a premium design and fill your CV in the builder.

2

Press Order

In the last step press Order / Payment Options, enter your name and phone, and submit. You will see the payment methods and your order number.

3

Pay and send the Transaction ID

Pay, then enter the Transaction ID. We verify manually.

4

Receive your access code

After we verify your payment we send the code by email and can also share it on WhatsApp. Enter it in the builder to unlock your premium PDF.

Payment verify hone ke baad code email ya WhatsApp par milta hai. Builder mein code daal kar PDF download karein.

Important: Your CV is saved in your own browser. Please do not clear browser data before downloading. If you change phone or clear data, contact us with your order number.

Tips for a better CV

Keep it to 1–2 pages. Use a clear photo with a plain background. Write results, not just duties. Add CNIC, date of birth or father’s name only if the employer asks. Check spelling before you print.
