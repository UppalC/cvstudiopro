UK CV format

A UK CV is usually short, plain and tailored to the job. Here is what UK university careers services recommend.

Updated November 2026 · Written by the CV Studio Pro team · All CV tips

Mukhtasar: UK CV aam taur par 2 page ki, bina photo, aur bina date of birth ke hoti hai. Har job ke mutabiq CV badlein.

The basics

  • Length: two pages is the standard in the UK, according to the University of Edinburgh careers service.
  • Photo: not needed. Leave it out unless the advertisement asks for one.
  • Personal details: include your name, address (or just your city), email, phone number and a link such as LinkedIn if it is professional. Date of birth, place of birth, gender and nationality are not needed.
  • Heading: use your name at the top. Do not write “CV” or “Curriculum Vitae” as the title.

Order of sections

  1. Personal profile (optional): two or three lines. Skip it if you have nothing specific to say. Avoid empty claims such as “I am hard-working”.
  2. Education, or employment first if you have a lot of experience.
  3. Employment and work experience, newest first, with short bullet points.
  4. Skills and interests, kept recent and relevant.
  5. References: “Available on request” is enough.

Style

Write without “I”: “Managed a team of five” reads better than “I managed a team of five”. Use a plain font, a clear layout and consistent spelling. UK employers expect British spelling, for example “organised” and “centre”.

Right to work

UK employers need to check that you have the right to work in the UK. If you are applying from abroad, or you need sponsorship, it is honest and helpful to say so briefly in your application or cover letter. Do not claim a status you do not have.

Tailor every application

Read the job description and use its key words where they truly describe your experience. A general CV sent to every employer is weaker than one adapted for each role. Our ATS CV checker can show which words from the advertisement are missing from your CV.

Where it differs from Pakistan or the Gulf

Pakistani and Gulf CVs often include photo, father’s name, date of birth, marital status and nationality. For a UK job you can normally leave these out. See CV format in Pakistan and CV format for the UAE and the Gulf to compare.

Source

Based on public advice from University of Edinburgh Careers Service: Build your CV. Other UK universities give similar advice, but employers differ.
