15 common CV mistakes and how to fix them

Most CVs lose attention for small, fixable reasons. Check your CV against this list before you send it.

Updated November 2026 · Written by the CV Studio Pro team · All CV tips

Mukhtasar: Spelling ki ghaltiyan, lambi CV, ghalat contact details aur har job ke liye aik hi CV sab se aam ghaltiyan hain. Bhejne se pehle is list se check karein.

Content mistakes

  1. One CV for every job. Change the summary and the skills to match each job advertisement.
  2. Listing duties, not results. Instead of “Responsible for sales”, write what you achieved, with a true number if you have one.
  3. Vague summary. “Hard-working person seeking a good opportunity” says nothing. Name your field, your years of experience and your strongest skills.
  4. Old, unrelated jobs in detail. Keep them to one line, or leave them out.
  5. Untrue or exaggerated claims. They can be discovered in an interview or a reference check.
  6. Unexplained gaps. If you have a gap, add a short honest line, such as study, family care or a course.

Layout mistakes

  1. Too long. One page for early careers, two for longer ones.
  2. Too crowded. Use clear headings, short bullet points and white space.
  3. Too many fonts and colours. One or two fonts and one main colour are enough.
  4. Tiny text. If you must shrink the text to fit, cut content instead.
  5. Skill bars and percentages for everything. They have no clear meaning. Use plain words instead.
  6. Text inside images. Software and some devices cannot read it. See our ATS guide.

Detail mistakes

  1. Spelling and grammar errors. Read it aloud, then ask someone else to read it.
  2. Wrong or unprofessional contact details. Check your phone number, and use a simple email address based on your name.
  3. Bad file name or format. Send a PDF named like Ali-Raza-CV.pdf, unless the employer asks for Word.

A 2-minute final check

  • Is the job title in my summary the job I am applying for?
  • Do my three most recent jobs each have clear, short points?
  • Can someone find my phone number in three seconds?
  • Is every fact true?
